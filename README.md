# The Sound of Identity

**Music, cultural geography, generational change, and social behavior: a global comparative review with Bangladesh as a case study.**

This repository maintains a working academic narrative-review manuscript and its supporting research protocol, evidence register, and research-gap analysis.

## Research scope

- Music, belonging, cultural identity, and local/global scenes
- Rock, pop, and other genre communities
- Age, cohort, and generational context
- Substance use and peer environments (association does not imply causality)
- Bangladesh as a focused comparative case

## Research status

**Work in progress.** This is an initial narrative review, not a completed systematic review or empirical survey. Source verification, screening, and fuller Bangladesh-specific evidence remain necessary before journal submission.

## Repository layout

- `paper/manuscript.md` — draft review
- `research/search-protocol.md` — preliminary and proposed search methods
- `research/evidence-register.csv` — source tracking
- `research/gap-analysis.md` — open research questions and limitations

Please cite primary literature rather than this draft. Contributions should include traceable bibliographic details and should not invent results, interviews, or data.
