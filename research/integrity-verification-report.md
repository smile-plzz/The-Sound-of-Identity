# Stage 2.5 Integrity Verification Report

**Manuscript:** *The Sound of Identity: Music, Geography, Generations, and Social Behavior in Global Comparison with Bangladesh*  
**Verification date:** 12 October 2026  
**Scope:** citation integrity, claim support, originality screening, internal consistency, disclosure, and research-audit completeness

## Verdict

**PASS WITH NOTES after correction round 1.** The pre-correction manuscript did not pass because it contained four material bibliographic defects and one uncited bibliography entry. All five were corrected before this verdict. No fabricated interviews, datasets, statistical results, or field observations were found. The paper remains a structured integrative review and a proposal for a future empirical study, not a report of completed primary research.

The notes are non-blocking: originality screening is heuristic rather than a commercial similarity report; several artist-specific passages are interpretive illustrations rather than findings from artist-focused scholarship; and one Bangladesh article has a valid DOI and journal volume but no independently confirmed issue number, so the issue number was removed.

## C. Internal consistency and research-status audit

## D. Originality and provenance screening

The manuscript was screened for suspiciously source-like phrasing and abrupt shifts in register. Representative passages from every major section were checked through exact-phrase web searches and against the cited source language available during verification. No strong evidence of unattributed verbatim copying was identified.

This is a bounded heuristic check, not a Turnitin or iThenticate report. It cannot rule out every phrase-level overlap. The user's earlier AI-assisted repository work was treated as discovery material and was not presented as independent empirical evidence. No raw AI output, fabricated quotation, interview, or invented dataset is attributed to a human source.

## E. AI-research failure-mode check

- **Fabricated source:** none remaining after DOI and metadata verification.
- **Real DOI attached to the wrong article:** detected once and corrected.
- **Bibliographic drift:** detected in two records and corrected.
- **Unsupported causal leap:** not detected in the final checked version; the paper repeatedly distinguishes representation, selection, association, mediation, and causation.
- **Invented primary data or participants:** none.
- **Results/proposal confusion:** none; the proposed Bangladesh study is clearly future work.
- **Overgeneralization from a genre to its listeners:** explicitly treated as the principal stereotype to avoid.
- **False comprehensiveness:** avoided; the limitations state language, database, regional, and method constraints.

## F. Residual limitations

The review is structured and auditable but not a database-complete systematic review. It is English-dominant, web-indexed, and multidisciplinary, which increases conceptual reach while limiting reproducibility compared with a preregistered single-database review. Bangladesh scholarship is uneven across genres, with stronger coverage of metal, nationalism, heritage, and protest than of mainstream pop, electronic scenes, rural listening, gender, and class outside Dhaka. The paper makes no population-prevalence claims about Bangladeshi listeners.
