# Reviewer Report and Revision Memo

## Simulated peer-review recommendation

**Recommendation: Major revision, followed by acceptance of the revised review as a conceptual integrative article.**

The manuscript addresses an ambitious and socially important question, connects fields that are too often separated, and makes Bangladesh analytically central rather than decorative. Its strongest contribution is the conditional mechanism model that links musical selection, identity work, performance, peer reinforcement, institutions, and political context. The paper is also unusually careful about the difference between representations of drugs or violence and evidence of behavioral effects.

### Major comments

1. **Clarify review status.** The search is transparent, but it is not exhaustive enough to be called a systematic review. Retain “structured integrative review,” state the limits prominently, and avoid PRISMA-style implications.
2. **Keep artist examples illustrative.** Nirvana, Pearl Jam, Audioslave, Dream Theater, and Nile are useful entry points, but the paper does not contain artist-specific audience studies. Their passages should be read as interpretive examples, not verified profiles of their listeners.
3. **Strengthen the global-to-Bangladesh logic.** The manuscript should continue to show what the Bangladesh case changes in theory: localized genre meaning, the role of linguistic nationalism, Dhaka-centered capital, and the coexistence of underground credibility with commercial aspiration.
4. **Preserve causal discipline in the conflict section.** Music may frame, synchronize, normalize, recruit, or intensify. It should not be described as independently causing war, genocide, protest, or peace.
5. **Separate the review from the proposed empirical study.** The proposed mixed-method design is valuable, but it must remain an agenda rather than a methods section for data that do not exist.
6. **Document citation repair.** Because several bibliographic defects were found, retain the integrity report with the public research package.

### Minor comments

- Avoid defining listeners through labels such as “gangster,” “dangerous,” or “passive.”
- Retain “gangsta rap” only as a historically situated subgenre term.
- Explain that the conflict-function table is conceptual.
- Do not add numerical prevalence claims about Bangladesh without a representative dataset.
- Keep the AI assistance disclosure and venue-policy caveat.

## Final editorial decision

The revised manuscript is suitable as a substantial initial academic manuscript and research package. It is not yet journal-formatted and has not undergone external human peer review. Before submission, the author should select a target journal, adapt length and citation style, and, if required, commission an independent similarity report and subject-specialist review.
